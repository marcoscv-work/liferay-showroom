Documentation and releases, no change to the assets. Fragments carry a `README.md` shown in their Docs tab, with live embeds of the fragment, of token tables and of page templates; three guide pages explain how to import, how tokens and style books fit together, and how to compose a page. Each release records a manifest of every asset's hash, so this page lists exactly what changed. The AI catalog (`catalog.json`, `llms.txt`) describes every fragment, template and style book for agents, and a bare `page-definition.json` dropped on the site becomes a template.
