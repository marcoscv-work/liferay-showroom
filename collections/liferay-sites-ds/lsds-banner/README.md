# Banner

A full-width strip with a title, a sentence and one action. It closes a page or breaks a long sequence of sections.

```fragment
key: lsds-banner
```

## Tone and alignment

`brand` and `accent` are both gradients and look alike on purpose: the design system keeps them for pages that alternate two banners. `neutral` is the flat version for quieter pages.

```fragment
key: lsds-banner
tone: neutral
align: left
```

Left alignment is for banners that sit under a left-aligned section; centred is the default closing banner.

## Writing

One sentence, one verb, one action. The rich text (`text-2`) accepts bold and links, but a banner with a paragraph in it has stopped being a banner.
